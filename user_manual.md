# FLOWER — User Guide for Mothers

Welcome to FLOWER. This is your digital pregnancy passport. You can use it to keep all your medical papers in one place, remember your checkups, and show doctors your health info quickly.

---

## What FLOWER does for you

- Keeps your pregnancy records safe in one place (ultrasound, blood tests, doctor notes, etc.)
- Reminds you of your next checkup
- Shows your profile (name, blood type, allergies, emergency contact) anytime
- Lets you call for help fast with the SOS button
- Works in English or Khmer — tap KH / EN to switch

---

## Before you start (first time)

1. Create an account or log in.
2. Fill in the Pregnancy Setup Wizard (weeks, height, weight, blood type, past pregnancies). This creates your profile.
3. After that, you see the 5 main tabs at the bottom.

---

## The 5 tabs (bottom bar — tap to switch)

### 1. Home
Your main screen. See your name, pregnancy week, and quick buttons to add a record or open emergency info.

### 2. My Records
Your medical document vault.
- **Add** — take a photo or upload a record from your doctor.
- **View** — open any record to see details.
- **Delete** — remove records you no longer need.

### 3. Calendar
Your checkup reminders.
- **Add** a new appointment (date, time, hospital, doctor name).
- **Edit** or **mark done** when you finish the visit.

### 4. Passport (Your Profile)
Your full profile page.
- See personal info, pregnancy info, and medical info.
- Tap the pencil to edit anything.
- Tap the QR button to open your emergency code for doctors / first responders.

### 5. Family Access
Add family members (partner, mother, friend) who can help you manage your data.

---

## Emergency help (SOS)

- Tap **SOS** in the header (or the bottom action bar) to open your Emergency Medical Card.
- It shows your blood type, allergies, conditions, medication, and emergency contact.
- Tap **Call Ambulance (119)** for quick help.
- The **QR code** lets a doctor scan and see your info instantly.
- Check the consent box to show the QR.

---

## Language

- Tap **KH** or **EN** at the top or bottom to switch between English and Khmer.
- All text, buttons, and messages will change.

---

## Logging out

- Tap **Logout** in the bottom action bar when you are done.
- Your data stays saved; you just end the session.

---

## Tips

- Add records right after each visit so nothing is lost.
- Check the Calendar so you do not miss appointments.
- Keep your emergency contact updated in Passport.
- If you switch phones, log in with the same account to see everything.

---

## Need help?

If something does not work, check:
- Your internet connection
- That you are logged in
- That you filled the Setup Wizard at least once
- The SOS button if you need urgent help
